Method and caveats
  • What each upper bound assumes (from analysis.oracle; see analysis/README.md):
  • selection: We decline (quote far above list) every order that lost money; nothing else changes. Costs are those that happened; declining costs nothing (a losing quote is free); dealer trust and the competitor's answer to fewer wins are ignored; the drivers freed by dropped orders are not reused. Cancelled orders are declined.
  • delivery: Every kept order arrives on time: no refund, penalty, fine, failed visit, waiting or release fee; the driver fees are the ones we paid. A driver was there in time for every kept order at the fee finally paid; notes are read perfectly. An order that was never delivered is costed at the week's paid fee ratio (undelivered_costed).
  • fee: Each gig that a driver completed is paid the lowest fee, as a share of the typical fee, that a driver completed a comparable gig for in the same world (same seed and level): the same car in any of our weeks (twin), or the same lane booked the same day for the same audience (lane). The driver who took the comparable could have taken this gig too, whatever the hour, deadline option and what drivers had learned; it is the lowest of several draws, so it flatters. A gig without a cheaper comparable saves nothing. Dropped bookings are not comparables.
  • quote_probe: A probe flip is the world's first answer: dealer trust 0, no late history, a competitor that has not answered our wins. Trust lets on-time weeks win above it later (understates); the competitor lowers prices for dealers we mostly win (overstates); neither is added: an order won above its flip counts 0 (won_above_flip). The probe's parent wins nothing, so the competitor raises its prices each morning and flips after Monday read high; the week figure extrapolates the probed window (extrapolated). Capacity is ignored and new_cost is the week's average paid fee ratio, not the marginal driver's fee: the loosest bound. An order the selection bound dropped is still repriced to its flip in full.
  • quote_brackets: An estimate, not a bound: the expected flip given our quote's outcome, from a win curve that pools all our weeks of that level and weekday (one priced option only). It knows neither this world's price level nor the war or outage day, and it contains the trust our own weeks built by that weekday. Lost quotes give a strict ceiling (lost_ceiling_cents); lost_below_similar measures how uneven the market is, not money.
